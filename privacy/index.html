Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

CrimeSafe ("we", "us") operates crimesafe.app, a free reference site for US crime and safety statistics. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit the site and how it is used.

Information We Collect

CrimeSafe does not require accounts, does not have login functionality, and does not ask you to submit personal information to use the site. We do not collect names, email addresses, or precise locations from visitors.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand aggregate site usage (pages visited, approximate region, device type). Google Analytics uses cookies and collects data as described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google and its partners may use advertising cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can manage ad personalization in Google Ads Settings, and learn more at How Google uses cookies in advertising. Users in the EEA and UK may see a consent prompt before personalized ads are shown.

Cookies

Cookies on this site come from the third-party services above (analytics and advertising). CrimeSafe itself sets no tracking cookies. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the site remains fully functional without them.

Data Sources

All crime statistics shown on this site are derived from publicly available government data, primarily the FBI Uniform Crime Reporting (UCR) program. No personal criminal records or personally identifiable information are published.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (e.g., GDPR in the EU/UK, CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. Since we do not collect or store personal data ourselves, such requests generally concern the third-party services listed above. For anything related to this site, contact us and we will help.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@crimesafe.uk or use the contact page.

Data is for informational purposes. Based on official city data and FBI UCR statistics. Verify with official sources for important decisions.
